Tickwork Light is the web version of Tickwork, an audio and MIDI sequencer coming soon to the Apple ecosystem. It runs in the browser with a limited feature set, so you can try the core idea of Tickwork before the full app is released.

**Status:** Tickwork is not released yet. Tickwork Light is a preview with fewer features than the upcoming Apple app.

## Try it

Open [Tickwork Light](https://lucarossid3v.github.io/tickwork-light) in your browser. No installation needed.
