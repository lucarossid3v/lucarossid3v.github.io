Welcome to **lucarossi.d3**, a place for notes on software, programming and technology.

## Who is writing

I'm Luca Rossi, a full stack developer and project manager. I have been building web software for more than 25 years, mostly in the Laravel/PHP ecosystem, and lately a lot of my work involves bringing LLM-based features into real products.

## What you will find here

- **Backend and web development**: PHP, Laravel, FilamentPHP, REST APIs, multi-tenant architectures.
- **AI in practice**: integrating LLMs, building assistants, and using AI tools in day-to-day development.
- **DevOps and tooling**: Docker, Git, CI/CD pipelines and the small automations that make a team faster.
- **Working in teams**: Agile, Scrum and the human side of shipping software.

## How the blog is built

The site is a static [Astro](https://astro.build) blog. Posts are plain Markdown files, so the content stays portable and easy to version with Git.
